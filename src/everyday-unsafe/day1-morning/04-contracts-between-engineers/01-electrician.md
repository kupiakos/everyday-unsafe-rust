# An Electrician Trusts the Label

- The electrician follows electrical code. The equipment they use and install is
  certified to operate within parameters. They trust an equipment manufacturer
  to correctly label a circuit breaker.

| Who         | Follows         | Trusts              |
| ----------- | --------------- | ------------------- |
| Electrician | Electrical code | The breaker's label |

<details>

- **Point:** nobody re-checks every part: they trust the label, and a wrong
  label is the labeler's fault.

- Nobody re-tests every part they install. They check the label, and the label
  is a promise from someone else.
- If a breaker labeled 20 A trips at 40 A, whose fault is the fire? The
  manufacturer's, not the electrician's.
- Ask: what's the "label" you trust most at work? (Usually: documentation.)

</details>
